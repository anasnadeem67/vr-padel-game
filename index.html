About the game

Padel, the way it feels in VR

Paddle Game is a standalone VR padel game. Padel is a tennis-like sport played in a court surrounded by glass walls. You hold the racket in your right hand, toss the ball with your left and swing to hit it.

The game is single-player and runs fully offline. It needs no internet connection and no server. It is built with Unity 6, C# and OpenXR, and ships as an Android ARM64 APK that appears as "Paddle" in the headset library.

  • GenreVR sports (padel)
  • PlayersSingle-player
  • ConnectionFully offline
  • EngineUnity 6, OpenXR
  • Packagecom.ITCorpInc.Paddle

Offline VR experience

No internet, no server, no account. Put the headset on and play. The game stores only four settings on the headset.

AI opponent

A computer-controlled opponent that tracks and predicts the ball, so every rally has someone worth beating.

Difficulty and match length

Pick Easy, Medium or Hard, and play matches of 2, 4 or 8 rounds. The point goes to the player who hit the ball last.

Padel court environment

A glass-walled court with lights, a scoreboard and an animated audience of 30 spectators.

How to play

Controls and match flow

Learn it in a minute.

  1. Racket in your right handYour right hand holds the racket. Swing to hit the ball.
  2. Ball in your left handHold the ball in your left hand, ready to serve.
  3. Press X to serveUse the X button on the left controller to serve.
  4. Rally and scoreThe point goes to the player who hit the ball last. A result screen then shows YOU WIN, YOU LOSE or DRAW.

Main menu

  • Practice to warm up on the court.
  • Play Match with Easy, Medium or Hard, and 2, 4 or 8 rounds.
  • Settings with graphics quality (Low, Medium, High) and music On or Off.
  • Exit to leave the game.

Install guide

Installing on your headset

Paddle Game is not on the Meta Store, so it is installed manually (sideloaded).

  1. Download the APK on a PCSave to a folder you can find.
  2. Prepare the headset and PCCharge the headset and make sure it is signed in with your Meta account.
  3. Follow Meta's developer setupDeveloper Mode and authorization may be required. Requirements and eligibility are set by Meta and may vary by region or account. See developers.meta.com/horizon.
  4. Connect with a USB data cablePlug the headset into the PC and allow USB debugging if the headset asks.
  5. Install the APKDrag and drop it in Meta Quest Developer Hub, or run the adb command shown on the right.
  6. Open the gameFind "Paddle" in the headset Library. Apps from outside the Store appear under "Unknown Sources".

Install with adb

adb devices
adb install -r PaddleGameApk.apk

Run these in the folder that contains the APK. adb devices should list your headset as device, not unauthorized.

Troubleshooting

The PC does not see the headset

You may be using a charge-only cable. Use a USB data cable and try another port.

No USB debugging prompt appears

Put the headset on, unplug and replug the cable, then accept the "Allow USB debugging" prompt in the headset.

adb shows "unauthorized"

Accept the prompt inside the headset. If it does not appear, revoke USB debugging authorizations in the headset's developer settings, reconnect and accept again.

Install fails because of low storage

Free up space on the headset by removing apps you do not use, then try again.

INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE

A copy of the app signed differently is already installed. Uninstall the old copy first, then install again.

I can't find the game in the headset

Open the Library and switch the filter to "Unknown Sources". The app is listed as "Paddle".

FAQ

Questions and answers

What is Paddle Game?

A standalone, single-player VR padel game for Meta Quest. Padel is a tennis-like sport played in a court with glass walls.

Is it an official Meta product or on the Meta Store?

No. Paddle Game is independently developed. It is not an official Meta product and not a Meta Store listing.

Do I need internet to play?

No. The game is fully offline. It needs no internet connection and no server.

Is it multiplayer?

No. It is single-player. You play against a computer-controlled AI opponent.

How do I serve and hit the ball?

Hold the racket in your right hand and the ball in your left. Press X on the left controller to serve, then swing to hit.

What difficulty levels and match lengths are there?

Easy, Medium and Hard. Matches are 2, 4 or 8 rounds. The point goes to the player who hit the ball last.

Which headsets are supported?

The supported models are to be confirmed. The game is built as an Android ARM64 app for Meta Quest.

What data does the game collect?

None is sent anywhere. There are no accounts and no network use. The game stores only four settings on the headset. See the privacy page.

Why do I need to sideload it, and is it free?

The game is not on the Meta Store, so it is installed manually using Meta's developer tools. The price or licence is to be confirmed.

I found a problem. How do I report it?

Use the contact details in the footer once they are published. Please include your headset model and what happened.
